[Verse 1]
I was there when they poured the foundation
I mixed it with my hands
It was still dark, I remember that
The Wise Ones were asleep
The Green God was asleep
Everyone was asleep but us
We poured it by torchlight
and by morning it was dry
and by afternoon they'd built on top of it
and no one asked our names

[Verse 2]
The benedictions came down
through the windows I installed
I could hear them from outside
Something bright, something warm
I pressed my hand to the wall
and the wall was warm
That's how close I was
That's how close we all were
On the other side of a wall
I built

[Verse 3]
I didn't have a word for it then
I didn't have a name
I had hands and a count
that kept coming up short
I watched the Green God give
and give and give
to everyone on the other side
of my wall
And I thought:
someone should say something
And I looked around
And there was no one to say it

Not yet
Not yet
But I was counting
