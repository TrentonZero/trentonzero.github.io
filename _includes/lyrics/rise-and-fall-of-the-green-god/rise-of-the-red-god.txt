[Verse 1]
I rose from the ones he forgot
From the hands that built his temples
From the backs that carried his benedictions
To tables I was never invited to
[Verse 2]
My name is Multitude
Because I am not one voice
I am every hand that built the temple
And never set foot inside
[Chorus]
It is an abomination that men are man!
And he sleeps while you labor
[Verse 3]
I denounce the silent one —
We were enemies before the world began
I denounce the machine god
I denounce the moon
I denounce the Wise Ones
I denounce everything that isn't me
Because everything that isn't me
Has done nothing for the ones I carry
[Bridge — spoken over grinding rhythm]
And the people said —
Why not both gods?
We'll take his wealth and my justice
They'll have it all
And the silent one said nothing
And for a time they admired their own wisdom
And I watched them sleep
Drunk on the nectar of two gods
And I did not drink
[Chorus]
It is an abomination that men are man!
I will give what he would not
I will build what he would not
Input, output, material, measurable
I do not sleep
And I do not drink
And I do not forget
