[Verse 1 — low chant over slow drums]
I wake
I wake and I am hungry
The Red God's hands have failed
And the world is full
And no one is watching the door
[Verse 2 — drums building, voices layering]
From the seas I drink
From the red fields I drink
From the cities where they placed their pride
I drink
I drink
I drink
[Chorus — full percussion, heavy guitar enters]
I walk openly upon the earth
For the first time since they cast me out
I walk openly
I am patient
I walk openly
I am patient
I have always been patient
I was patient before the world was made
And now the waiting is done
[Verse 3 — the rhythm deepens, more voices join the chant]
The followers of the silent one — thin
I choke on them
The followers of the moon — poison
I choke on them too
And drink more
There is always more
[Bridge — the drums drop to a single slow beat, spoken low]
They rose from their mourning
Late
Too late
And found me standing in the open air
With the earth in my claws
And I was not hiding
I was not whispering
I was just eating
[Final Section — all percussion unleashed, the acceleration hits, chanting layered on chanting]
And when all is drunk
And nothing more remains
I drink the nothing too
I drink the space where the world was
I drink the memory of the world
I drink until there is nothing left
But me
And him
And his silence
